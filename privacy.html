Privacy Policy

Effective 5 August 2026

ValleyMind AI ("ValleyMind", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who else touches it, and the control you have over it. It applies to the ValleyMind AI web app and any related services we operate.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address
  • Username and profile details
  • Authentication tokens and session data

Content you create or upload

  • Images, videos, audio and documents you upload
  • Prompts, generated images, generated video and generated text
  • Chat history, where you have chat memory enabled
  • Project files, saved assets and Knowledge Base documents

Technical and usage information

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address (used for security, abuse prevention and rough region detection)
  • Feature usage and generation counts (needed to enforce plan limits)
  • Error and performance logs
  • Cookies and local storage

We do not collect payment card details. Payments are handled by our payment processors, who receive what they need to complete the transaction.

2. Why We Collect It

  • To deliver the AI features you ask for
  • To save your projects and sync them across your devices
  • To enforce free and paid plan limits fairly
  • To improve speed, quality and reliability
  • To personalise your experience, including persona memory
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud and misuse of the service
  • To provide customer support and respond to your messages
  • To meet our legal obligations

3. Your Content Is Yours

  • You own what you create on ValleyMind AI, subject to the terms of the underlying AI providers and applicable law.
  • We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.
  • We do not use your private projects or uploads to train our own models without your explicit, separate opt-in.
  • You can delete individual projects, chats and assets at any time.
  • You can delete your account entirely — see Section 7.

We may access account content only where strictly necessary: to fix a fault you have reported, to investigate a credible abuse or safety report, or where the law requires it.

5. Security

We protect your data with:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all traffic
  • Hashed credentials and token-based authentication
  • Access controls limiting who and what can reach stored data
  • Storage on managed infrastructure with its own security controls
  • Ongoing security review and improvement as the platform grows

We do not claim end-to-end encryption. Your content is encrypted in transit and at rest by our storage providers, but ValleyMind and the AI providers listed above can technically process it in order to deliver the service. No online service is perfectly secure, and we won't pretend otherwise.

If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by law.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to:

  • Keep you signed in between visits
  • Remember your preferences (theme, language, active persona)
  • Cache assets so the app loads faster
  • Understand aggregate usage so we know what to improve

You can clear or block cookies in your browser, but signing in and staying signed in will not work properly without them.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You can, at any time:

  • Access and download the data associated with your account
  • Correct your profile information
  • Delete individual chats, projects and uploaded assets
  • Delete your account and the content stored with it
  • Manage cookies through your browser
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Object or complain about how we handle your data

To exercise any of these, use the controls in Settings or email supportvalleymind@gmail.com. We respond to requests within 30 days.

Account deletion removes your content from active systems. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten, and anonymised, non-identifying usage statistics may be retained.

If you are in Nigeria, you have rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. If you are in the EU/UK, you have equivalent rights under the GDPR.

8. Data Retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. Content is kept until you delete it or close your account. Logs and security records are kept for a limited period appropriate to their purpose, then deleted.

9. Children's Privacy

ValleyMind AI is not intended for children under 13. If you are between 13 and 18, you may only use ValleyMind AI with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if we learn that we have, we will delete it. Parents or guardians who believe a child has created an account should contact supportvalleymind@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as ValleyMind AI grows or as our providers change. The "Effective" date at the top always reflects the current version. For material changes — new categories of data, new purposes, or a new class of third-party processor — we will notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
