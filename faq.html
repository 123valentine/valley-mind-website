FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ValleyMind AI.

What is ValleyMind AI?
ValleyMind AI is an intelligent all-in-one AI platform. In a single workspace you can chat with AI, generate images and video, build websites, write and code, edit photos and video, use AI voice, analyse files and store your projects — built for creators, with African creators in mind.
Is ValleyMind AI free?
You can start for free. ValleyMind AI offers free and paid plans, with local pricing paid in Naira. Paid plans lift usage limits and unlock more capacity.
What can I create with ValleyMind AI?
AI chat, images, video, a website, written content, code, graphic designs, edited photos and videos, and voice — plus file analysis, project management and cloud storage. See the Features page for the full list.
Who are Marcus, Elena and Angelina?
They're ValleyMind's three AI personas, each with their own voice, temperament and strengths. You can switch between them mid-conversation, or bring all three into the same thread to work through a problem together.
Is my data private? Do you train models on my content?
Your work is yours. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your private projects or uploads to train our own models without your explicit, separate opt-in. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
What languages does ValleyMind AI support?
ValleyMind works in our existing languages today, and support for Igbo and Nigerian Pidgin is on the roadmap.
What devices does it work on?
ValleyMind AI runs in the browser and works across your devices, and it's engineered to run lightly on mid-range phones. You can also install it as an app from a supported browser.
Can I upload documents and work with them?
Yes. You can upload documents and work with them directly — ask questions, summarise, and extract information through the file-analysis feature.
How do I pay?
Payments are in Naira and handled securely by our payment processors (Paystack / Flutterwave). We don't store your card details.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes. You can delete individual chats, projects and assets at any time, or delete your account entirely from Settings. See section 7 of the Privacy Policy.
How do I get support?
Email supportvalleymind@gmail.com or reach us on the Contact page. We respond to data requests within 30 days.

Still have questions?

Reach out any time — we'd love to hear from you.
